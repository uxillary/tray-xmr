## Strategy and decision basis

Make Ember useful at the point a reader is confused: compare like-for-like measurements, understand what a log signal establishes, and distinguish operating estimates from payments. Existing tools already include calculation provenance, hypothetical arithmetic and local processing; existing Huge Pages guidance already distinguishes privilege/allocation. Prefer a better decision path to more pages.

The 52 candidate queries cover four clusters and join to 17 shared task profiles. Official evidence (T), community anecdotes (C), competing offers (R), search samples (S) and hypotheses (H) are separately recorded in the research document. Strongest missing need: connection/share/payout interpretation (C04–C07/C13/C14 with T07/T10/T17). Comparable performance and whole-PC efficiency are also supported (C02/C08–C10). Exact-query demand for hardware purchase economics, tariffs and simulations remains weaker.

No confirmed public origin, search console or organic baseline exists. No traffic/ranking forecast, measured keyword demand, competitor audience estimate or causal backlink promise is made. Twelve competitor resources were investigated; official documentation, benchmark provenance and existing calculator assumption controls are strengths to respect. Presentation alone cannot establish ranking advantage.

## Scoring model

All scores are **editorial assessments**, not measured ranking predictions. Each criterion is 1–5. Equal weights make trade-offs inspectable; close totals are not empirically meaningful.

| Code | Criterion | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| N | Evidence of user need | 1 speculative; 3 adjacent questions; 5 repeated directly relevant questions/strong technical task. Anecdotes are not population frequency. |
| G | Existing resource quality gap | 1 already served; 5 consequential missing decision support. Includes existing Ember coverage and competitor strengths. |
| O | Organic discovery relevance | 1 weak landing intent; 5 coherent search task. Does not imply query volume. |
| D | Distinctiveness | 1 duplicates existing offers; 5 useful original method/decision experience. |
| C | Technical credibility | 1 insufficient source/model basis; 5 strong authoritative basis for the bounded proposal. |
| U | User usefulness | 1 peripheral; 5 directly resolves task. |
| V | Visual/interactive potential | 1 plain text sufficient; 5 useful comparison/diagram/reference. A high score does not mandate JavaScript. |
| L | Internal-link value | 1 isolated; 5 resolves a current contextual handoff. |
| Q | Potential citation value | 1 generic; 5 traceable method/reference/original evidence worth citing. No backlink prediction. |
| E | Implementation effort (cost) | 1 small copy/table change; 3 several-day scoped content/tool work; 5 substantial data/model/validation work. Relative estimates, not quotes. |
| M | Maintenance burden (cost) | 1 stable units/text; 3 periodic upstream review; 5 continuous data/version/policy upkeep. |
| R | Technical/safety risk (cost) | 1 minimal; 3 hardware/complex-model/security context; 5 substantial unresolved risk. |

**Total / 60 = N + G + O + D + C + U + V + L + Q + (6 − E) + (6 − M) + (6 − R).** Higher benefits and lower costs rank higher. Sort descending by total; ties use stable backlog ID ascending only, not a claimed difference in expected value. Type and execution gates override numerical position: unresolved rights, hardware authorization or model safety cannot be waived by a high score.

Disposition types: **1** improve existing page; **2** expand existing guide/tool explanation; **3** new standalone guide; **4** reusable reference; **5** interactive tool; **6** original research; **7** defer due to complexity/risk/maintenance. New/expanded items are proposals, not shipped resources.

## Selected priorities

### Five strongest near-term content opportunities

1. **B02 — Whole-PC wall-power method (54):** expand ELECTRIC's existing measurement help with meter boundary, matched interval, total versus incremental consumption and a compact record. Cite actual methods; publish no unmeasured device values.
2. **B03 — Comparable-run record (53):** strengthen LOW's existing small measurement record with algorithm/version, configuration, window, allocation and power context, plus a hypothetical labelled comparison. Existing checklist remains; do not imply it was absent.
3. **B01 — Connection/share/payout decision guide (52):** one substantive troubleshooting resource, with anchors for no connection, rejected submission and accepted-but-no-payment. Link primary pool/P2Pool policies rather than prescribe generic fixes.
4. **B04 — Difficulty and expected rewards (52):** one Learn explanation distinguishing network/job/sidechain difficulty and expected value from actual payment; connect to existing profitability input help.
5. **B05 — Diagnostic reference (51):** finite static source-versioned table linked from the decoder; state recognition versus reference-only coverage. New filtering and parser expansion are separate work.

These are the highest-scoring content/reference items; interactive and research items are selected separately. B20 benchmark literacy is valuable later but overlaps LOW's near-term comparison example; link upstream records instead of copying them.

### Three strongest interactive opportunities

**B06 efficiency comparison**, **B07 diagnostic reference filtering**, **B12 deterministic difficulty/reward teaching controls**, in that execution order. B07 depends on a sufficiently useful static inventory; B12 depends on B04 and comprehension testing. Neither needs another generic calculator or a URL per parameter. See the interactive document for formulas, edge cases, no-JS alternatives and privacy boundaries.

### Two strongest research opportunities

**B08/E01 threads versus sustained H/s and total wall watts** and **B09/E02 paired idle/active wall power**. B09 scores slightly higher for effort; E01 supplies the most direct explanatory performance comparison, while E02 validates its power boundary. Conduct together on one owner-operated PC only after explicit authorization, suitable metering and protocol review. No results exist.

### Deferred work and explicit reopening gates

- **B23 CPU explorer:** documented API exists, but dataset reuse/caching/redistribution terms, service limits and comparable metadata must be resolved. An official software GPL license does not license service records. No scraping/import in M13B.
- **B24 configuration assistant:** validate a distinct task beyond the official wizard, a versioned schema subset, address/pool checks, safe defaults and export/security boundaries.
- **B25 full variability simulator:** independent mathematical review and mode-specific model needed; do not apply a solo Poisson model to P2Pool payment windows.
- **B22 upgrade economics / “best CPU” lists:** requires traceable comparable measurements and explicit manual purchase/running-cost assumptions. No hardware shopping or ROI recommendation.
- **B21 deep P2Pool installation:** high version/node/privacy/operations burden; B01 handles the immediate payment distinction and links upstream. A deeper guide waits for a distinct need and expert review.
- **B18 tariff automation, further experiments E03–E07 and new taxonomy:** weak exact demand or greater burden; start with static methods/owner-run baseline. No supplier feeds, live pricing or decorative mining simulation.

## Recommended M13C implementation sequence

**Proposed initial scope: B02 + B03 + B01 only** — two focused existing-resource expansions and one new substantive diagnostic guide. This is a practical bounded first package, not authorization to implement it during M13B.

2. **B02 measurement method:** improve ELECTRIC's existing note/semantic explanation, with measured-versus-estimated and total-versus-incremental boundaries. No power figures or device recommendation without data; no tool math change.
3. **B03 comparison record:** extend LOW at the existing measurement step; link to B02 and original benchmark method. Add only a clearly synthetic example or empty record, not invented research.
4. **B01 diagnostic guide:** source each stage against pinned XMRig output and actual pool/P2Pool docs. One URL with connection/rejection/payment anchors; no pool ranking or universal stale-share fix. Link from SETUP, DECODER and TROUBLE at the matching state.

Recheck production-origin/noindex/privacy/product disclosures. No deployment or promotion without separate authorization.

**Next package, separately scoped:** B05 static reference and B04 difficulty explainer, followed by B06 efficiency comparison after UI/formula review. B07/B12 wait for demonstrated comprehension/navigation need.
