## Cross-page quality notes

- The strongest current quality pattern is a qualified answer, explicit limitations, primary XMRig/Microsoft/Monero citations, and contextual links to a next useful step.
- No source code or browser crawl was used to claim rankings, indexing, impressions, or query volume. This repository snapshot does not establish deployed canonical URLs.
- Article publication dates are 6 October 2026 in the inspected collection sources. Product status has a separate evidence basis; content dates are not a substitute for source review.
- Accessibility remains unfinished: M10B records that dedicated screen-reader testing and measured contrast testing have not been completed. Keep this as a separate QA backlog item, not an implied SEO pass.

## Consolidation decisions

Do not consolidate the RandomX memory/cache, CPU-thread, Huge Pages, and low-hashrate pages merely because they share concepts. Their needs differ: conceptual model, thread choice, a specific Windows permission/allocation symptom, and broad performance diagnosis. Cross-link them at the point where one explanation hands off to the next. The calculator pair also serves distinct needs: electricity-only operating cost versus manual expected-value scenario.
