## What makes a resource worth sharing

The strongest contribution is a specific solved problem: distinguish a miner submission from a payout-qualifying share, compare measured whole-PC energy under controlled conditions, or document a reproducible null result. Include enough explanation in the contribution that a reader can assess it without clicking. Offer source/method links for verification, disclose the Ember relationship, and make the independent resource usable without installing Ember or providing contact information.

Keep a stable canonical public URL and dated method/revision history when publication is separately authorized. Sources should be linkable at meaningful sections. A raw-data release needs consent/redaction/license review from the [experiment programme](m13b-original-research-programme.md); do not redistribute benchmark-service records under an assumed license.

Potential reciprocal value is technical correction, evidence or a reusable local tool. Do not promise backlinks, follow status, rankings or traffic as a condition of helping. Paid placements, automated posting, fake accounts, undisclosed self-promotion, mass submissions, keyword-stuffed replies and artificial engagement are excluded.
